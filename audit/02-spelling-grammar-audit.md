## Style Preferences — Not Errors

These four were listed as errors in the original audit. They are defensible edits, but the existing text is not incorrect. Apply them if you want the house style; skip them without loss.

| # | Location | Current text | Suggested | Why it is not an error |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Footer | "**COPYRIGHT** (c) 2026" | "Copyright (c) 2026" | All-caps is a typographic choice. Consider `©` rather than `(c)` if changing it anyway. |
| 8 | Footer | "...improve accessibility as **our** collective moral obligation" | — | **The original fix was wrong.** It proposed "its collective moral obligation", which reads worse — "collective" implies a group, and "its" narrows it to the company. This is standard UserWay boilerplate referring to society at large. Leave as written. |
| 10 | Footer | "...also for those **of us with** disabilities" | "...also for people with disabilities" | Inclusive-language preference. Both forms are grammatical and both are in common use. |
| 12 | Footer | "...**makes available** the UserWay Website Accessibility Widget" | "...provides the UserWay..." | "Makes available" is correct English. "Provides" is shorter. |
